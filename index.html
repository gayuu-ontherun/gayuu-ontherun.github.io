About Me

Hi, I'm Gayatri! I left home when I was 19, and honestly, life has been quite an adventure since then.

For the last 5 years, I’ve been travelling solo, exploring new places, meeting all kinds of people and figuring things out along the way. I’m someone who enjoys slow travel, little mountain villages, local experiences, long bus journeys and sometimes just going somewhere without having everything planned. I’ve also spent time volunteering, which has let me experience places and connect with people in ways that regular travel sometimes doesn’t.

A little while ago, I started documenting my travels and creating content. It’s become a lovely way to keep these memories alive and share them with others.

I also started hosting group trips and have hosted 6 so far. There’s something really nice about bringing a bunch of strangers together and watching them turn into a group of friends. There’s still so much I want to see, experience and learn. For now, I’m just enjoying the journey and seeing where it takes me!

Frequently Asked Questions

I'm travelling solo. Is that okay?

Absolutely! Most of my travellers come solo. You'll be added to the group chat before the trip so you can get to know everyone, and on the trip you'll share a room with another traveller of the same gender.

What is included in the trip price?

Stays, transport during the trip, the meals mentioned in the itinerary, local guides, entry fees and activities listed for each trip. personal expenses and anything not listed are not included.

How big are the groups?

Groups are kept small — usually 16 to 20 people — so everyone gets to know each other and the trip stays relaxed.

Is it safe for women travellers?

Yes. Safety is my top priority. I personally check every stay and route, work only with trusted local partners, and I'm with the group for the entire trip.

How do I pay, and when is the balance due?

Pay a 50% advance by UPI or bank transfer to confirm your seat. The remaining amount is due before the trip starts.

What if I need to cancel?

Please see the cancellation policy in the "How to Book" section above. If you can't make it, you can also transfer your seat to a friend at no extra cost.

What should I pack?

You'll get a detailed packing list for your trip once your booking is confirmed. It covers clothing, medicines, documents and anything special for that destination.
